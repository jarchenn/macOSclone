Aurora is a small desktop operating system built from scratch for x86-64. The desktop is its own program: a display server composes the windows of separate app processes, and every app can crash without taking anything else down.

Getting around:
- Click an icon in the dock to open Terminal, Files, Notes or Settings.
- Drag a window by its title bar, resize it from the lower-right corner. The red, amber and green buttons close, minimize and maximize it.
- Alt+Tab switches windows. Click a dock icon to restore a minimized app.
- Notes opens and saves through file dialogs; Files and the Terminal (try: ls, cat, help) show the same files.
- Settings changes the theme, the screen resolution and the keyboard layout.
