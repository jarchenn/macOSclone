Aurora is a small desktop operating system built from scratch for x86-64. Its kernel, drivers, window manager and apps all run inside QEMU on emulated hardware.

Getting around:
- Click an icon in the dock to open Terminal, Files, Notes or Settings.
- Drag a window by its title bar. The red, amber and green buttons close, minimize and maximize it.
- Ctrl+Tab switches windows. Click a dock icon to restore a minimized app.
- Notes saves to /home/notes. Files and the Terminal (try: ls, cat, help) show the same files.
